Appearance

Backup

Export regularly. Data stays in this browser and does not sync across devices. Image attachments are included in exports.

Reminders

Allow notifications to receive a reminder when RecallFlow is open. A browser-only PWA cannot reliably schedule notifications while fully closed; dependable closed-app reminders require a native Android build with local-notification support.

Revision schedule

Initial: 1, 2, 4, 7, 14, 21, 28 days. Maintenance: 1, 2, 3, 4, then 6 months between confident checks. Red recall retries in 2 days; partial recall retries in 14 days.
